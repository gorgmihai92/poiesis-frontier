# First principles

Break the problem into the parts that must be true, list the assumptions, and remove the ones that are not needed.

1. **The goal, without the solution.** State what has to be achieved without naming how it is achieved now
   ("people find the action they need", not "a better navigation menu").
2. **What must be true.** List the conditions any solution has to meet. For each, say where it comes from: a
   tested insight, a source (note and page), a physical or legal limit, or "assumed".
3. **The assumptions.** List what the current way takes for granted (about users, materials, costs, order of
   steps, who does what). Ask of each: what if it were false?
4. **Remove.** Strike every assumption that is not required by step 2. For each one struck, write the concept that
   becomes possible without it.
5. **Rebuild.** From what is left, build two or three concepts that meet step 2 in a way the current solution
   does not.

What remains assumed after step 4 is the assumption the experiment tests. Each concept goes through
`playbooks/experiment.md`.
