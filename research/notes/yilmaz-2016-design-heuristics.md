## What it says (from the abstract)
- The question: how product designers create several concepts to consider.
- Four empirical studies are combined: award-winning products, many concepts for one project by an experienced
  industrial designer, and sets of concepts from 48 industrial and engineering designers for one design problem.
  Together, 3,457 concepts.
- From the variations seen across them the authors extract 77 "Design Heuristics": patterns of how designers
  appear to introduce intentional variation into product concepts.
- The abstract says the heuristics "can help designers generate more, and more varied, candidate concepts".
