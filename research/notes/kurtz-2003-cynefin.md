# The new dynamics of strategy: Sense-making in a complex and complicated world (Kurtz & Snowden, 2003)

- **Source:** IBM Systems Journal 42(3): 462–483 · https://doi.org/10.1147/sj.423.0462
- **File:** none; no open copy. Read: the abstract (OpenAlex). Pages unconfirmed.
- **Licence:** closed.
- **Evidence:** analytical (a framework from action research).

## What it says
- Challenges three assumptions common in decision support and strategy: order, rational choice and intent
  (abstract).
- Presents the Cynefin framework, a sense-making device for the complexity that appears when those assumptions are
  relaxed, derived from several years of action research (abstract).

## What it means for the plugin
- Before generating concepts, frontier asks what kind of problem it is; ordered problems do not need frontier.
- The domains and their decision models are taken from van Beurden et al. 2011, which was read in full.
