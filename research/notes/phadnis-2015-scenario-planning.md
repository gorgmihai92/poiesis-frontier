# Effect of Scenario Planning on Field Experts' Judgment of Long-Range Investment Decisions (Phadnis, Caplice, Sheffi & Singh, 2015)

- **Source:** Strategic Management Journal 36(9): 1401–1411 · https://doi.org/10.1002/smj.2293
- **File:** none here (an author's manuscript is kept locally in poiesis-insight; pages = PDF pages)
- **Licence:** author's manuscript: local only.
- **Evidence:** empirical · three field experiments with experts judging long-range investments (p. 1).

## What it says
- Using multiple scenarios did not raise or lower experts' overall confidence; judgments moved with how an
  investment fared in the scenario used; experts then preferred more flexible options (p. 1).

## What it means for the plugin
- One scenario steers judgment toward itself. An assumed future is built as at least two contrasting ones, so a
  speculative artefact is not read as a prediction.
