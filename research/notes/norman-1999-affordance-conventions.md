  Pages unconfirmed.

## What it says
- There are real and perceived affordances, and the two need not be the same.
- In screen-based interfaces, all the designer controls is perceived affordances; conventions (learned, shared by a
  cultural group) matter more than affordances there.
- Constraints are physical (close to real affordances), logical (reasoning narrows the options) and cultural
  (learned conventions).

## What it means for the plugin
- The concepts playbook separates what a thing allows, what people perceive it allows, and the convention that
  makes them read it so, before looking for other forms of the same purpose.

## Quotes
- "In graphical, screen-based interfaces, all that the designer has available is control over perceived
  affordances." (1998 post)
