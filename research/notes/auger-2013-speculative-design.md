## What it says
- Speculative design serves two purposes: to help us think about the future, and to critique current practice
  (abstract).
- A key concept is the "perceptual bridge", the means by which a design engages its audience (abstract).
- Success depends on carefully managing the speculation: what informs the technology, aesthetics, behaviour,
  interaction and function of the artefact (abstract).

## What it means for the plugin
- The speculative playbook names each artefact's purpose (future or critique) and manages what makes it plausible,
  so people engage with it instead of dismissing it.
