# Sources behind the rules

The sources behind the rules (each rule names its source in `skills/frontier/rules.md` and the playbooks). One
note per source in `notes/`; texts in `papers/` stay local and are not in the repository. Each note has a
**Read** line: the full text, part of it (which part), the abstract only, or the title only, and the date.

- **What kind of problem it is:** [Kurtz & Snowden 2003](notes/kurtz-2003-cynefin.md), the Cynefin framework
  (abstract); [van Beurden et al. 2011](notes/vanbeurden-2011-cynefin.md), the domains and probe, sense, respond.
- **Leverage points:** [Meadows 1999](notes/meadows-1999-leverage-points.md), the twelve places to intervene;
  [Abson et al. 2017](notes/abson-2017-leverage-points.md), four groups, and leverage points interact.
- **Analogies:** [Gentner 1983](notes/gentner-1983-structure-mapping.md), relations, not attributes;
  [Gick & Holyoak 1980](notes/gick-1980-analogical-problem-solving.md), analogies used mostly when prompted
  (abstract); [Chan et al. 2015](notes/chan-2015-near-far-inspiration.md), closer sources gave more creative ideas
  (authors' summary; to read in full).
- **What a thing is for:** [Norman 1999](notes/norman-1999-affordance-conventions.md), real and perceived
  affordances, conventions.
- **Speculative design:** [Auger 2013](notes/auger-2013-speculative-design.md), the perceptual bridge (abstract);
  [Phadnis et al. 2015](notes/phadnis-2015-scenario-planning.md), several scenarios, not one;
  [Mancini et al. 2010](notes/mancini-2010-contravision.md), two contrasting versions of one story (full text);
  [Elsden et al. 2017](notes/elsden-2017-speculative-enactments.md), staging with people who are in on it (part);
  [Coulton, Lindley & Akmal 2016](notes/coulton-2016-design-fiction-deception.md), plausible fiction slides into
  deception (full text).
- **Speculative research:** [Alvesson & Sandberg 2011](notes/alvesson-2011-problematization.md), five kinds of
  assumption to contest (the method section); [Blythe 2014](notes/blythe-2014-imaginary-abstracts.md), imaginary
  abstracts (full text); [Chambers 2013](notes/chambers-2013-registered-reports.md), title only, nothing rests on it.
- **Evaluation that fits the stage:** [Greenberg & Buxton 2008](notes/greenberg-2008-usability-evaluation-harmful.md),
  usability testing of early ideas (full text); [Olsen 2007](notes/olsen-2007-evaluating-ui-systems-research.md),
  evaluate against the claim (full text).
- **Generating:** [Card, Mackinlay & Robertson 1991](notes/card-1991-design-space-input-devices.md), design spaces
  (full text); [Bell, Blythe & Sengers 2005](notes/bell-2005-making-strange.md), defamiliarisation (part);
  [Djajadiningrat, Gaver & Frens 2000](notes/djajadiningrat-2000-relabelling-extreme-characters.md), relabelling
  and extreme characters (full text); [Pierce & Paulos 2014](notes/pierce-2014-counterfunctional-things.md),
  counterfunctional things (full text); [Uzzi et al. 2013](notes/uzzi-2013-atypical-combinations.md), conventional
  work with an unusual pairing (full text); [Yilmaz et al. 2016](notes/yilmaz-2016-design-heuristics.md), abstract
  only, nothing rests on it.
- **Distinct ideas, and ideas on paper against ideas carried out:** [Si, Yang & Hashimoto 2024](notes/si-2024-llm-research-ideas.md)
  and [Si, Hashimoto & Yang 2025](notes/si-2025-ideation-execution-gap.md) (both in part).
- **Small studies:** [Tohidi et al. 2006](notes/tohidi-2006-testing-many-is-better.md), one design against three
  (full text); [Dow et al. 2010](notes/dow-2010-parallel-prototyping.md), parallel prototyping (full text);
  [Lakens 2022](notes/lakens-2022-sample-size-justification.md), what a sample can detect (part);
  [Nielsen & Landauer 1993](notes/nielsen-1993-finding-usability-problems.md), seeing a problem at least once
  (part); [Eypasch et al. 1995](notes/eypasch-1995-zero-events.md), none of n is not zero (abstract);
  [the G*Power manual](notes/gpower-2023-manual.md), how power is computed (documentation, three sections).
- **Convincing is not evidence:** [Tversky & Kahneman 1983](notes/tversky-1983-conjunction-fallacy.md), the
  conjunction fallacy (reference only).
- **Experiments fixed in advance:** [Nosek et al. 2018](notes/nosek-2018-preregistration.md), prediction and
  postdiction, preregistration (part).
- **Why the gate and `power.py` exist:** [two recorded runs](notes/poiesis-lab-2026-recorded-runs.md) in the
  owner's lab notebook, observed in part.

**Methods used, sources not yet read:** de Bono (lateral thinking: provocation, reversal), Dunne & Raby 2013
(*Speculative Everything*). They are used as methods; no rule rests on a claim from them. The published critiques
of speculative design the lab plan names (Bardzell & Bardzell 2013, Pierce 2021, Tonkinwise 2014) were not read.
